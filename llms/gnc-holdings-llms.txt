# GNC Holdings

> GNC Holdings, LLC (General Nutrition Centers) is an American specialty retailer of health,
> wellness and performance products — vitamins, minerals, herbal supplements, sports nutrition,
> diet and energy products. Founded in 1935, headquartered in Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania, and a
> wholly owned subsidiary of Harbin Pharmaceutical Group since its 2020 acquisition. GNC operates
> company-owned and franchised retail locations, online stores and retail partnerships across
> roughly 50 countries.

IMPORTANT — no public API. As of 2026-09-12, GNC Holdings publishes **no public developer program,
no API documentation, no machine-readable contract (OpenAPI / AsyncAPI / GraphQL / gRPC / WSDL),
no MCP server and no A2A agent card**. `api.gnc.com` and `developer.gnc.com` do not resolve; the
full `/.well-known/` discovery surface, `/apis.json`, `/openapi.json` and `/llms.txt` return 404 on
every host GNC operates. Its storefront runs on Salesforce Commerce Cloud (Demandware) and its
store locator on Yext, but neither vendor contract is published by GNC for third-party use.

This file is written and maintained by API Evangelist (https://apievangelist.com) as a third-party
profile. It is NOT published by GNC Holdings and does not represent the company.
